Steps 2–5 — Install it

  1. Install a free signer on your computer
    Use Sideloadly (Windows/Mac) or AltStore. Connect your iPhone/iPad with a USB cable.
  2. Sideload the IPA
    Open the signer, drag DangmeKeyboard-unsigned.ipa into it, sign in with your Apple ID, then press Start. When asked for a password, use an app-specific password from appleid.apple.com.
  3. Trust the certificate on your device
    Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID, then Trust.
  4. Add the keyboard
    Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard… and select Dã́ngme Keyboard. Open any app, tap a text field and hold the 🌐 key to switch to it.
⏳ With a free Apple ID the signature expires after 7 days. Reconnect the device and press Start again in Sideloadly/AltStore to renew — your keyboard and settings stay installed.
